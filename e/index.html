A few good questions.

When will it ship?

Expected early December 2026. We'll keep you updated as we get closer. Your pre-order is fully refundable before shipping.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. A full refund any time before shipping. Reply to any email from us.

Is it always listening?

Only when listening is on. You can pause it any time, and delete what it keeps. It has no camera.

Do I need an iPhone?

Yes. It pairs with your iPhone over Bluetooth and uses it to reach the internet.

Can ChatGPT or Claude use it?

Yes. Connect ChatGPT, Claude or Poke, and they can ask what Jalapeño knows about your day. You approve each connection and can revoke it any time.

Is it a hearing aid?

No. It sits behind your ear and leaves it open, so you hear everything around you. It's an earpiece you talk with.

Is it waterproof?

Water resistance is in validation. We'll share the rating before it ships.

Will it listen to my TV?

It's built to tell the people you're with from what's playing, and we're still improving it.

What comes with it?

One Jalapeño wearable, with Moonshot's voice. Your iPhone provides the internet connection.

What about privacy?

Audio is processed online for conversation and memory. Our privacy policy explains how it works. Read it here.

How does the friend discount work?

After ordering, you'll get your own link. Your friend saves $10 when they use it. You get $10 back when they order.
