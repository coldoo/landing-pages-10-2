Only when listening is on. You can pause it any time, and delete what it keeps. It has no camera.

Yes. It pairs with your iPhone over Bluetooth and uses it to reach the internet.

Why we built it

Dylan's grandmother quietly stopped taking her blood pressure medication. She fell cooking and hit her head, and her husband called Dylan's dad instead of 911 for half an hour.

At the hospital, nobody could tell the doctor her medication, and finding the pills took two more hours. She never woke up.

In Dylan's words: "One piece of information, two hours sooner, and we could have done everything." We built Jalapeño, an assistant you wear that hears your day and speaks up before you ask.

The moonshot was never Mars. It is a single human life, fully lived, fully felt, and not forgotten.
